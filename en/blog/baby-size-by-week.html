Baby size by week and your due date

October 8, 2026 · 6 minute read

The sweetest answer to "How big is the baby now?" is a fruit. Below you can first work out which week you are in and your estimated due date. Then you will find the baby's approximate length, weight and fruit comparison from week 4 to week 40.

Due date and pregnancy week calculator

The estimated due date is traditionally found by adding 280 days (40 weeks) to the first day of your last menstrual period. Pregnancy weeks are counted from the same date. Your doctor may update the date after an ultrasound; the date your doctor gives you always comes first.

Choose a date.

Only a small share of babies are born exactly on their due date. Babies born from week 37 onwards are considered full term, so it is more accurate to think of the due date as a window of a few weeks rather than a fixed day.

The three trimesters

  • First trimester (weeks 1–13): the foundations of the organs form. By the end the baby is about the size of a peach.
  • Second trimester (weeks 14–27): you start to feel movements and growth speeds up.
  • Third trimester (weeks 28–40): the baby gains weight and gets ready for birth.

Baby size by week chart

The values are averages and every baby grows at their own pace. Until week 20 length is usually measured from head to bottom and after that from head to heel, which is why there is a jump between weeks 19 and 20. The fruit comparisons are rough images to help you picture the size.

Sharing the weeks with your loved ones

Many families send a message every week: "This week we're the size of a lemon." Instead of writing it every week, you can show it on a page automatically: when your loved ones open the link they see the baby's size this week, the countdown to the due date and your weekly notes.
